Third-party apps
Slack/Teams/email cards with deep links

  

  

  
Alerting design rules (guide §6): alert on negative transitions only (failure and recovery, not every green run); one alert per failure type; every alert links to the run and names the failing job; escalate by severity — prod hand-offs page humans, CI noise gets a Slack thread.
