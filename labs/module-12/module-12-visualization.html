12.1 Keyed vs keyless — why OIDC wins (ADO: service connection secret → workload identity federation)

Click each approach to see what a single leaked credential looks like — and why the keyless model has nothing to steal.

Keyed — SPN secret in CI
One long-lived password, reused on every run, rotated manually.
Keyless — OIDC federation
A signed per-run token; Azure validates identity claims; no cloud secret stored.
